# Credits

Thaumaturge Tech Compats is an addon for Thaumaturge. It does not include any model, sound or code of Thaumaturge: it only uses Thaumaturge through its API and by referencing its content at runtime. It does include recolored versions of some Thaumaturge textures, described under "Thaumaturge" below.

Paths are relative to `src/main/resources/assets/thaumaturgetechcompats/`. Everything not listed under "Avaritia" or "Thaumaturge" below is original work made for this project.

## Avaritia, by Spiteful_Fox

Thank you to **Spiteful_Fox** for creating **Avaritia**, and for its Thaumcraft integration, which this mod takes as its starting point to bring that content to **Thaumaturge**.

Avaritia: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/avaritia
Source: https://github.com/SpitefulFox/Avaritia

What comes from Avaritia's Thaumcraft integration (`fox.spiteful.avaritia.compat.thaumcraft`):

* The **Terminus** aspect and its icon.
* The **Akashic Records** item, its icon and its idea.
* The **Extremely Primordial Pearl**, its animated icon and its infusion.
* The **Infinity wand rod** and the **Crystal Matrix wand cap**, with their wand textures.
* The Infinity wand recipe in the Extreme Crafting Table.
* The tab icon (`ascension`) of the Avaritia research category.

## Thaumaturge, by Leclowndu93150, Iglee42, Saereth, Reclipse, DeadSix and Azanor13

This mod needs **Thaumaturge** to run. Its 3D models (including the meshes of the smeltery, node stabilizer, node transducer, workbench charger, mnemonic matrix, warded jar and alchemical furnace) and sounds belong to Thaumaturge and to the artists it credits in its own `CREDITS.md`. They are not included here: the blocks of this mod point to those models by name.

Thaumaturge: https://github.com/Leclowndu93150/Thaumaturge

**Recolored textures.** The textures of the matrix, neutronium and infinity tiers (blocks, items, wand parts and golem materials) are recolors of the corresponding Thaumaturge textures, so that they match its style. The author, Leclowndu93150, was asked on Discord whether recoloring Thaumaturge assets for an addon is a problem, and answered "no".

These textures are derivative works of Thaumaturge's art, which belongs to its authors (Leclowndu93150 and the artists credited in Thaumaturge's `CREDITS.md`). They are **not** covered by this project's MIT License and remain subject to their authors' rights. If you are an author of the original art and want any of them removed or changed, open an issue and it will be done.

## Solar Flux Reborn, by Zeitheron

The four Thaumcraft solar panels (alchemical, alchemical brass, thaumium and void metal), their names, their generation, transfer and capacity values and the structure of their research chain follow the Thaumcraft compatibility of **Solar Flux Reborn**, made by **Zeitheron** (original Solar Flux by **Nauktis**). This mod registers them through the public API of Solar Flux Reborn and reuses the panel textures that Solar Flux Reborn ships. No code of Solar Flux Reborn is included.

Solar Flux Reborn: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/solar-flux-reborn
Source: https://github.com/dragon-forge/SolarFluxReborn
